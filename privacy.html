Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information The Dark Protocol ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website or buy one of our digital products, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Questions: support@thedarkprotocol.com.

What we collect

  • Purchase details. When you pay, PayPal processes your payment. We receive your name, email address, PayPal order ID, the product and the amount. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Delivery email. We use your PayPal email address to send your access link and code.
  • Browser storage. This site stores your order ID, purchase date and email in your own browser (localStorage) so the thank-you and upgrade pages can recognise your purchase. This data stays on your device and you can clear it at any time.
  • Usage data. Page views and checkout events are measured with the Meta Pixel (see "Advertising" below). Our hosting provider records basic technical logs such as IP address and request time.

How we use it

  • To confirm your payment with PayPal and deliver the product you bought.
  • To answer support requests and process refunds.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.
  • To prevent fraud and unauthorised access to paid content.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Services that process your data

  • PayPal: payment processing.
  • Brevo: sending the access email.
  • Gumroad: hosting and delivering the course files (you enter your email on Gumroad to download).
  • Netlify and GitHub Pages: website hosting and payment verification.
  • Meta (Facebook): advertising measurement.
  • YouTube and Google Fonts: the embedded video and the site fonts are loaded from Google servers.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. Some of them are located outside your country, including in the United States.

Advertising and cookies

We use the Meta Pixel, which sets cookies and sends events such as page views, checkout starts and verified purchases to Meta. Meta uses this to measure our ads and may use it to show you ads. You can control ad personalisation in your Meta ad settings, block third-party cookies in your browser, or use an ad blocker.

How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as needed to provide access, handle refunds and meet tax and accounting obligations. Support emails are kept while the conversation is active and for a reasonable time afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to its use for advertising. Email support@thedarkprotocol.com from the address you used to buy. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Age

Our products are for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
